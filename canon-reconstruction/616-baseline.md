# 616 Reference Baseline
## Marvel Earth-616 Status Quo Relevant to *Grandmaster × Salvage*

This document establishes what published Marvel Earth-616 canon says about the characters, organizations, and events that appear in the Salvage campaign. It is organized as an educational reference — explaining not just *what* things are but *why* they are that way.

Each entry is sourced. Claims from web-verified published sources are cited. Claims drawn from training-data knowledge are tagged:
- `[616-CONFIDENT]` — well-established, multiply sourced
- `[616-PROBABLE]` — consistent with known canon but not directly verified this session
- `[616-UNCERTAIN]` — plausible but unverified; flag for audit

**This baseline establishes what was true in 616 *before* your story began.** Your story's deviations from this baseline are not errors — they are either player-declared facts (Tier 1 authority), GM world-building (Tier 3), or genuine canon drift to be resolved.

## Part 1: What Era Does This Story Take Place In?

### The Short Answer

Your story most closely matches a **post-Krakoa, early-Fall-of-X era of Earth-616**, likely circa 2023–2024 in publication terms, with elements borrowed freely from the broader 2019–2024 Hickman-influenced period. It is *not* rigidly tied to any single published storyline — it is a composite drawing on:

- The **Krakoan Age** (2019–2024): Established by Jonathan Hickman's *House of X / Powers of X* — a period when mutants formed a sovereign nation-state on the living island Krakoa, developed resurrection technology, produced pharmaceutical drugs that gave them UN recognition, and repositioned themselves as cosmic players.
- The **post-Secret Empire** period (2017+): After Hydra's most dramatic power play — a mind-controlled Steve Rogers temporarily seized the United States using a cosmic cube — Hydra was broken but not permanently destroyed.
- The **Cosmic Reconstruction era** (post-Annihilation, Annihilation Conquest, War of Kings — roughly 2006–2012 in publication): The Kree Empire, Guardians of the Galaxy, and cosmic power dynamics your story draws on were largely defined here.
- The **Al Ewing cosmic era** (2020–present): SWORD, the Guardians' Galactic Council deputization, and the interweaving of cosmic and mutant concerns.

**Your story diverges from all of these in significant ways** — most notably, Hydra has been *permanently eliminated* (which never happened in published 616) and a single OC has reorganized global power to an extent no canonical character has achieved. These are Tier 1 player decisions and Tier 3 GM world-building, not 616 continuity errors.

## Part 2: Status Quos in Effect

### Mutants & Krakoa

**The background:** In 2019, Jonathan Hickman rewrote the X-Men's premise entirely. Professor X and Magneto — former enemies — united to establish **Krakoa**, a living island that became the first mutant nation-state. Krakoa produced psychoactive drugs with unique pharmaceutical properties (one extended human lifespan, one cured mental illness, one treated diseases resistant to all other treatment), and the UN recognized Krakoa as a sovereign nation in exchange for access to these drugs. Mutants gained diplomatic immunity, ambassadors worldwide, and gateways allowing instant travel.

More critically: Krakoa developed **resurrection protocols**. Via a network of five mutants called the Five (Goldballs, Proteus, Hope, Elixir, Tempus), combined with Professor X's Cerebro that stored every mutant's psychic backup, Krakoa could reliably resurrect dead mutants. Death became something mutants could survive.

**Why this matters for your story:** Your story features resurrection pods as tech. In 616, resurrection was Krakoa's defining political and social technology. Your story's resurrection capability likely draws from this concept, either as looted tech or GM-originated world-building. `[616-CONFIDENT]`

**Current status (Fall of X):** By 2023–2024 in publication, Krakoa fell due to **Orchis** — an anti-mutant organization that engineered a catastrophe at the Hellfire Gala, using a sleeper agent to trigger a mass attack that scattered mutants worldwide. Krakoa as a functioning nation is destroyed by the time the *Fall of X* arc concludes. `[616-CONFIDENT]`

**Source:** [Krakoa — Marvel Database](https://marvel.fandom.com/wiki/Krakoa_(Earth-616))

### SHIELD

**The background:** SHIELD (Strategic Homeland Intervention, Enforcement and Logistics Division) is Earth-616's primary espionage and superhuman monitoring agency. It has been dismantled, replaced, and rebuilt multiple times. Nick Fury Sr. discovered that SHIELD had been infiltrated by Hydra since its founding — a revelation from Hickman's *Secret Warriors* (2009–2011). SHIELD was later replaced by HAMMER under Norman Osborn during the Dark Reign era, then rebuilt. It has also been intermittently shut down by the US government and reconstituted.

**Why this matters:** Your story portrays SHIELD as an early partner that lost coordination authority at Week 82. This is tonally consistent with 616 SHIELD's recurring pattern of diminished authority — in published canon, SHIELD regularly loses power, gets compromised, or is replaced. The specific "lost coordination authority" beat is GM world-building, but SHIELD being outmaneuvered is canonical in spirit. `[616-CONFIDENT]`

### Hydra

**What 616 says:** Hydra is one of Marvel's oldest and most resilient villain organizations, founded during WWII and repeatedly rebuilt after apparent destruction. Their motto — "Cut off one head, two more shall take its place" — is both flavor and mechanical truth. Hickman's *Secret Warriors* (2009–2011) inflicted their most devastating canonical defeat: Nick Fury discovered SHIELD had been Hydra's puppet since founding, destroyed Hydra's main base (Gehenna), and killed Baron Strucker. The High Council disbanded. **But Hydra rebuilt.** They returned in *Secret Empire* (2017) as a fully reconstituted organization that briefly conquered the United States via a mind-controlled Captain America.

**Story deviation — FLAGGED:** Your story shows Hydra being **permanently and completely eliminated** via Salvage's drone campaign (Week 95). In published 616, Hydra has never been permanently destroyed — it always reconstitutes. This is either:
- A Tier 1 player declaration (if you explicitly decided Hydra was done)
- A Tier 3 GM world-building decision
Either way, it is a significant departure from 616 canon. In your story's universe, this elimination may stick — but you should know that "Hydra stays dead" is not a 616-canonical outcome.
