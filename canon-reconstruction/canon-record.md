## Part 1 — Core Immutable Facts

### The Power
- **Canonical name at Week 0:** "The Looting Power" (player-described in session setup)
- **Canonical name current:** "Combat Alchemy" (see `context-files/combat-alchemy.md`)
- **Granted by:** The Grandmaster via Iso-8 integration
- **Mechanism:** Iso-8 fragment bonded to Salvage's neurological and metaphysical structure at Week 0. Stabilized approximately Week 6.
- **Iso-8 dual function:** (1) powers the generation system; (2) broadcasts a metaphysical frequency that registers to hostile entities as opportunity/challenge/prey — causing threats to seek Salvage out. Salvage does not know this.
- **Grandmaster framing:** A "self-perpetuating game" — can the game escalate organically rather than through managed brackets? Salvage is the game board.

### Salvage's Knowledge State (permanent through Week 163)
Salvage does NOT know:
- That the Grandmaster exists or granted his power
- That Iso-8 is involved
- That the threat-attraction mechanism exists
- That cosmic entities have re-evaluated his classification
- The full scale of cosmic attention on him

Salvage DOES know:
- His power's mechanics (as described in `context-files/combat-alchemy.md`)
- That he is the most powerful individual on Earth (self-aware by Week 82)
- His drone force, fleet, infrastructure — all operational details
- His political constraint: he must maintain the faith of his people

### Superseded Mechanics
- **Intent Responsiveness:** Original Week 0 power description included a mechanic where Salvage could "focus intent" to influence generation direction. Player cut this. Current canon: the power assesses and generates entirely automatically. Early-session references to directed intent are `[SUPERSEDED]`.
