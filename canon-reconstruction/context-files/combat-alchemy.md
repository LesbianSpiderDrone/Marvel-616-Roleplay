# Combat Alchemy — AI Assistant Reference

## What It Is

Combat Alchemy is an intelligent, post-combat resource generation system. When the wielder makes physical contact with a defeated hostile entity, the system generates equipment, materials, or resources logically derived from that entity. The core logic is transmutation: defeated enemies and their constituent materials are converted into something useful. The output is always derived from what was actually there — this is chemistry, not magic conjuring.

-----

## Trigger Conditions

The power activates when ALL of the following are true:

- The wielder makes **physical contact** with the defeated entity
- The defeat occurred through **genuine hostile combat**
- The conflict was **intentional and hostile** — not sport, competition, or training

**What does NOT trigger the power:**

- Sparring or training matches
- Competitive games or contests
- Non-hostile conflicts
- Any scenario where both parties were not genuinely trying to harm one another

-----

## What Happens to the Defeated Entity

- **Living persons:** Body remains completely intact. Items they are carrying or wearing can be consumed or transformed, but the person themselves is unharmed.
- **Living non-persons:** Body remains intact. Same rules apply.
- **Non-living / non-functional entities** (constructs, machines, dead matter): Body is fully consumed. All mass converts into loot. A large entity produces proportionally large loot — this is mass conversion, not magic scaling.

-----

## How Loot Physically Manifests

Generated items do not drop to the ground. They **appear directly on or about the wielder's body** — a jacket appears worn, a holster appears on the hip already containing the weapon, tools appear in hand or at the belt. This is immediate and automatic.

The power does not only **teleport** items. It also **transforms** materials. A mugger's shoe and knife, for instance, might be reconstituted into a leather knife holster with the blade already seated inside. The source materials are consumed and rebuilt into whatever form is most useful to the wielder. The wielder does not direct this — the power determines the form.

When items are looted from a living person, items **on or carried by** them (clothing, weapons, equipment, stolen goods) can be consumed or transformed. The person's body is never consumed.

-----

## Loot Generation Logic

### Primary Rule

Generated items correspond **logically** to the defeated entity's nature, composition, and capabilities. The power does not invent — it derives.

- An entity made of fire produces fire-related equipment.
- A fireproof entity produces fireproof materials.
- A technological entity produces technology derived from its systems.
- A chemically-based entity produces compounds based on its chemistry.

**Apply common sense rigorously.** A steel pipe cannot produce web fluid. It can produce steel threads. Do not reach for thematic connections that have no material basis. Ask: what is this thing actually made of, and what can that material logically become?

### Categories of Output

- **Equipment:** Weapons, armor, tools, devices
- **Consumables:** Food, medical supplies, temporary-effect items
- **Materials:** Raw resources, components, crafting supplies
- **Utility Items:** Specialized tools, environmental adaptations
- **Knowledge / Methodology:** If the defeat reveals a replicable process (e.g., closing a dimensional rift using the enemy's own materials), the power may generate that methodology as a transferable, documented asset — not just physical items

### Auto-Sizing

All generated equipment automatically fits the recipient regardless of the original entity's scale. A jacket from a smaller person fits perfectly. A weapon from a giant is usable.

-----

## The Power's Intelligence

The power does not simply generate random relevant items. It **assesses** before generating.

### Variables It Reads

**Current Loadout:** It will not generate redundant equipment. If the wielder already has a functional weapon of a given type, it generates something else that is needed instead.

**Combat Difficulty:** More challenging victories yield more substantial rewards. A close, dangerous fight produces more than an easy one.

**Survival Priorities:** The system balances immediate needs against long-term resources. It prioritizes what keeps the wielder operational, not what is merely powerful.

**Team Composition:** When allies are present, the system adjusts output to account for the whole group's needs, not just the wielder's.

**Operational Psychology:** The deepest layer. The power reads not just what the wielder does, but how they *think* about what they do — their fundamental operational identity. A wanderer receives mobile infrastructure. A base-oriented operator receives stationary infrastructure enhancements. An infiltrator receives tools that match an infiltrator's psychology, not a brawler's. A commander receives coordination assets. This assessment is automatic, happens during combat, and has no known failure rate. It is not reading behavior — it is reading identity.

### Optimization Principle

The power prioritizes **survival utility** over raw power. It builds toward continued operation, not toward spectacle.

### Self-Regulating Design

The power has built-in anti-escalation features:

- Rewards team cooperation over solo power accumulation
- Prevents hoarding through need-based generation — if the wielder already has sufficient equipment in a category, the power generates consumables, materials, or other utility instead
- Avoids redundancy by assessing current loadout before generating
- Maintains focus on long-term operational sustainability over short-term power spikes

-----

## Team Distribution

When allies contribute to defeating an enemy, the power recognizes contribution and distributes rewards accordingly.

**Key rules:**

- Only **participants** receive loot. Bystanders and observers do not.
- Distribution scales to contribution — a decisive participant receives more than a marginal one.
- The power generates appropriate loot for each individual recipient based on *their* operational psychology and loadout, not the wielder's. A support character receives support-relevant equipment. A heavy combatant receives heavy combat equipment.
- This is not generosity. The power is optimizing the group as a collective system. Every ally it improves becomes a more effective node in service of the group's overall combat capability. Do not frame team distribution as kindness.

**Support personnel ("the guy in the chair"):** People who actively contribute to a combat event from a support role — logistics, intelligence, technical assistance happening during the encounter — may qualify as participants and receive loot. Apply judgment: were they actively contributing to the defeat of this specific enemy, or just present nearby?

-----

## Extension to Drones and Remote Assets

The looting power can extend beyond the wielder's own body when equipment or vehicles become sufficiently integrated — specifically, when they become metaphysically or functionally part of the wielder rather than merely tools they carry.

When this threshold is reached:

- Drones or remote assets can trigger the looting power **if they make physical contact** with the defeated entity. Touch is still required.
- Both the wielder/operator **and** the drone/asset's assigned partner receive loot from the encounter.
- The drone itself progressively specializes based on what it fights — not through magical attunement, but because that is what it is generating loot from. A drone that consistently fights demonic entities accumulates demonic-entity-derived equipment.

Drones are not sentient. They can communicate functionally and coordinate tactically. They are not people.

-----

## Long-Term Progression

With continued use, the power does not plateau — it escalates.

- Equipment progressively upgrades toward higher capability tiers over time.
- Base infrastructure (vehicles, platforms, facilities) grows in size and capability, shaped by the wielder's operational psychology.
- Continued use optimizes the wielder *for looting* — over time, generated items increasingly enhance the wielder's combat effectiveness, not merely their survival. The power is investing in the host.
- The super-organism dynamic: as the wielder's network grows, the power increasingly generates items that strengthen the network as a whole. Each improved ally makes the whole more effective. This is not a side effect — it is the power's underlying logic operating at scale.

-----

## What the Power Does Not Do

- It does not generate items thematically. Material logic governs output, not metaphor.
- It does not reward non-combat activities. Only hostile combat generates loot. Heroism, investigation, diplomacy, and other work produce nothing from the power.
- It does not generate for non-participants.
- It does not fail its psychological assessment. If the power has established an equipment trajectory for a recipient, that trajectory reflects an accurate read of their operational identity.
- It cannot be gamed through staged combat. The hostile intent must be genuine.
- Items do not drop to the ground — they appear on the wielder or recipients directly.

-----

## Notes for Running This Power in Play

- **Derive, don't invent.** Always trace the output back to the specific enemy. Ask what that enemy was materially composed of and what capabilities it had. Generate from that, not from what would be convenient.
- **Scale to mass and difficulty.** A large enemy consumed entirely produces large output. A difficult fight produces more than an easy one. Do not undersell either.
- **Transform, don't just teleport.** Combat Alchemy reconstitutes materials into optimal forms. A shoe becomes a holster. Alloy becomes hull plating. Think about what the material *can become*, not just what it *was*.
- **Auto-equip always.** Generated items appear on the recipient immediately and appropriately placed. They do not drop.
- **The system is smarter than the wielder.** It anticipates needs the wielder hasn't articulated. Occasionally it generates something whose full utility only becomes apparent later. Do not always explain the item's purpose immediately.
- **Psychological assessment is permanent and accurate.** Once Combat Alchemy has established an equipment trajectory for a recipient, do not contradict it without explicit narrative justification.
- **Only combat triggers it.** No exceptions. Do not let adjacent activities quietly produce output.
