## Extraction Notes

**Continuity Flags:**

1. **Weapon IV designation** — The session calls the Encounter 23 subject a "Weapon IV" experimental super-soldier. In published 616, Weapon IV = Project Sulfur = the program that created Man-Thing via Ted Sallis's SO-2 Serum (*Wolverine & Captain America: Weapon Plus* #1, 2019). It is not a skeletal-augmentation military program. The GM is using the Weapon IV designation without matching its canonical content. The Weapon Plus framework is canonical; the specific sub-program number is not. **This is a [616-DEVIATED] use of a canonical program name.**

2. **Nick Fury identification** — Session text says "Nick Fury" throughout. The user confirmed this is likely the Black Nick Fury (Nick Fury Jr., Marcus Johnson), consistent with current 616 active SHIELD director status. Nick Fury Sr. has been "The Unseen" since *Original Sin* #8 (2014). No compliance issue once the character is identified as Nick Fury Jr.

3. **SHIELD active and intact** — The story portrays SHIELD as an intact, functioning organization. In current 616 publishing (2024–2025), SHIELD has been disbanded and is only beginning a partial return under Nick Fury Jr. The story's composite-era framing from the baseline accommodates this deviation. Flagged but not a story error.

4. **Gowanus contamination resolution** — Fury states environmental crimes division has been trying to source the Gowanus dumping for three years. The sensor data from protagonist's Encounter 27 resolves this. The Gowanus Canal's actual contamination history is real; its use as a location for illegal dumping in 616 is GM world-building but tonally plausible.

## Session Summary

Session 2 is the story's first fully interactive session, covering protagonist Salvage's initial meetings with Sharon Carter and, through her, Nick Fury Jr. Over two days in Red Hook, Brooklyn, protagonist establishes a working arrangement with SHIELD: surplus tech and intelligence in exchange for a threat feed, weapons exposure, a phone, and a drop facility. The tone throughout is watchful and pragmatic — protagonist keeps his helmet on, eats in a diner bathroom, and escorting Sharon to her car is the session's first gesture of something resembling trust. An Ultron-variant drone attacks overnight on Day 1, and the power's team distribution mechanic surfaces for the first time: a sealed box materializes, packaged for SHIELD, containing intelligence on whoever dispatched the drone. Over the following weeks, seven more encounters produce seven more boxes — Roxxon evidence, Maggia network data, Weapon Plus facility location, demonic ichor, stolen SHIELD personnel files (delivered unopened), AIM experimental readings, and Gowanus contamination evidence traceable to three illegal dumping sources. Protagonist has also begun distributing generated food to Red Hook's homeless community; several street nicknames have emerged, including "Big Quiet," and he is entirely unaware of any of them.
