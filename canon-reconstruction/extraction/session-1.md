## Extraction Notes

1. **"2012 invasion / Chitauri" inconsistency** — The Chitauri are canonically from Earth-1610 (Ultimate Universe). Their use as "leftover from 2012 invasion" maps the MCU Battle of New York (2012, Chitauri) onto Earth-616 where the analogous event used different aliens. This may reflect the GM blending MCU framing with 616 setting. **Flag for Phase 2: establish whether this story's 2012 invasion was Chitauri (MCU-derived) or another 616 event.**

2. **MODOC vs. MODOK** — "Mental Organism Designed Only for Computation" appears to be a GM-fabricated acronym variant, not a published 616 designation. The published 616 variants are MODOK (Killing), MODAM (Aggressive Maneuvers), and alternately MODOC (Conquest — an alternate-universe MODOK title). The "Computation" variant is not found in published sources. The concept (failed AIM prototype) is plausible; the designation is fabricated.

3. **Sharon Carter described as "late twenties"** — Sharon Carter in 616 has decades of field experience and an ambiguous but clearly substantial age. "Late twenties" may be a visual/narrative shorthand, not a canonical age claim. Low-priority issue.

4. **Power generates "valid insurance documents"** — This is an unusual abstract application. The power's stated rules allow for "legal contracts" from appropriate source types (Damage Control is a legally operating company). The philosophical question of whether the power can generate genuine legal documents (as opposed to forgeries) is left open.

5. **The Grandmaster's three metrics** — Survival probability (71% by Week 16), escalation sustainability, equipment optimization — these are GM-generated data points that will be worth tracking across sessions to see if they trend meaningfully.

6. **No in-scene player dialogue yet** — The entire session is setup + montage. No real-time interactive scene between protagonist and any character has occurred. Session 1 is purely framework-establishing. The interactive story begins at Session 2 with Carter's approach.

## Session Summary

Session 1 functions as both a rules-establishment session and an extended compressed prologue. The player authored a detailed power system ("The Looting Power") before roleplay began, then declared that the Grandmaster granted this power via an Iso-8 fragment, making the protagonist an unwitting piece in a self-perpetuating cosmic game. The GM established the Grandmaster's perspective as a primary narrative layer — an ancient cosmic being monitoring a living "Battleworld" from a pocket-dimension sanctum, tracking three data streams. Over the following 16 story-weeks, the protagonist survived 19 hostile encounters solo, building from a civilian with a malfunctioning tech suit to a fully self-sufficient operator driving a 25-foot armored mobile fortress with a comprehensive arsenal and months of supplies. The session ends as Sharon Carter, SHIELD Enhanced Individual Liaison, makes first contact at Red Hook, Brooklyn — standing 40 feet from the protagonist's vehicle, tablet raised, claiming to know why threats keep finding him.
