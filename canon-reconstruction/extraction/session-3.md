Session Log: Grandmaster x Salvage 3.md
Session Number: 3
In-story Timeframe: Week 20 (Day 1) through approximately Week 24, Day 8 post-framework
Real-world Session Date: [Not recorded]
Extractor Notes: Session 3 is the longest session to date (12,287 source lines). It covers
five major structural arcs: (1) Weeks 20–21 routine encounter montage with SHIELD power
scaling analysis; (2) Week 23 Zarathos-Nar Omega-level battle and group loot distribution
event — the first large-scale demonstration of the power's team-arming capability; (3) Avengers
integration via unanimous vote and Tony Stark deal, followed by WSC COSMIC TOP SECRET
briefing; (4) Guest Combatant Framework development — "loot farming" defined, negotiated,
and approved, with Steve Rogers pitching to Xavier and Richards; (5) Salvage's in-person
visits to Xavier Institute (Day 4 post-framework) and Baxter Building (Day 5), culminating
in Negative Zone incursion pre-briefing (Day 8). Session ends mid-scene.
The session introduces the Suit AI / Integrated Support System as a speaking character for
the first time. It also introduces the Avengers as a full ensemble, the X-Men as institutional
partners, and the Fantastic Four as institutional partners. Grandmaster's internal metrics
appear at regular intervals and are a primary tool for tracking story-state.
Information asymmetry note: No NPC knows that Grandmaster exists, or that Iso-8 is the power
source, or that threat attraction is a designed mechanism. These facts are known only to
Grandmaster and the reader. Salvage does not know about threat attraction; he believes he is
"just always busy."
